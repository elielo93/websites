FAQ

Leaf Guards FAQ

Do gutter guards really work in Florida?

Quality micro-mesh guards do. Cheap foam and plastic screens fail against palm fronds and pine needles. We only install products we've seen hold up through hurricane seasons.

Will I never have to clean my gutters again?

You'll go from twice a year to a quick visual check every few years. Debris that lands on the mesh dries and blows off.

How much do gutter guards cost?

Typically $8 to $14 per linear foot installed, depending on product and gutter size. Bundle with new gutters and save.
